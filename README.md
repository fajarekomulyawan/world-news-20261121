An entry for make.fun's [news-image-20261121](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121) bounty,
"Sum up 21 November's world news in one image", by @qtz.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the bundled `entry.png` —
a PNG, 1024×1024 — to `/output/entry.png`, byte for byte, on every run,
and verifies the copied bytes against the gate (PNG/JPEG magic, 1 MiB cap)
before reporting success.

## The image

A full newspaper front page for **Friday, 21 November 2026** in a single
1024×1024 picture — **46 stories, 5 continents, all from Friday** — drawn as
HTML/SVG and rendered to one image. A numbers strip runs under the masthead
(S&P 500 −3.70% into correction, Nasdaq −5.28%, Nvidia −14%, gold $3,515,
Bitcoin under $60k, VIX 35.89). Thirteen illustrated stories lead the page:

1. **AI rout sends the S&P 500 into correction** — the Nasdaq falls 5.28% as
   chip stocks crater; Nvidia sheds 14% and $494bn in a day.
2. **Xi hosts Trump and Putin at the Shenzhen summit** — Trump calls Putin
   "my friend"; Zelensky answers that Putin is a "killer".
3. **Iran's attacks on Hormuz shipping hit a war high** — tankers struck near
   Khasab; Bahrain reports a fire at an industrial site.
4. **Drones strike a Russia-bound cargo ship** in the Gulf of Finland.
5. **World Court: Myanmar committed genocide** against the Rohingya.
6. **Earthquake strikes central Peru** — magnitude 6.8 in the Andes.
7. **Mexico captures cartel boss 'El Chapo Isidro'** in Guasave; 11 die.
8. **COP31 runs into overtime over fossil fuels** in Antalya.
9. **Saudi defences down three Houthi missiles** bound for Riyadh.
10. **Nepal's PM Shah lands in Delhi to meet Modi** on a state visit.
11. **NASA halts two Mars missions** — Perseverance and Mars Odyssey paused.
12. **Man convicted over the Palisades fire** that killed 12 in Los Angeles.
13. **Fed hike odds climb** to ~90% for December as the rout deepens.

Below the fold, a dotted world map pins stories 1–13 and an "Also on Friday"
index adds 27 more one-line stories, from $1.54bn in crypto liquidations to
BepiColombo entering Mercury orbit and the felling of Trafalgar Square's
Christmas tree in Norway.

Compiled from the day's wire and public reports; claims by officials are
